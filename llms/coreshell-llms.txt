# Coreshell

> Coreshell (Coreshell Technologies, Inc.) is a San Leandro, California battery technology
> company founded in 2017. It develops silicon-anode lithium-ion battery cells built on
> low-cost, domestically sourced metallurgical-grade silicon instead of graphite or refined
> polysilicon, stabilized by a proprietary nanolayer surface coating, and has unveiled
> commercial-scale 60 Ah cells for electric vehicles, defense platforms and stationary
> energy storage.
